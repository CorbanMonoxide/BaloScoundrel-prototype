# Scoundrel Consumables

Consumables are single-use or temporary items that offer immediate tactical advantages or survival utility during a run. They can be purchased in the [[Scoundrel Shop]] or drafted from Chests.

## Rarity Tiers

### Common
*   **Potions**: Standard healing items to restore base HP. (These are part of the base deck, but additional Potions can be acquired and permanently added to the player's deck).

### Uncommon
*   **Smokescreens**: Allows the player to hide one monster for the current room. 
    *   *Mechanic*: The hidden monster counts as "removed" for the sake of clearing the room (meaning the player only needs to resolve 2 out of the 4 cards to advance). However, the hidden monster *will appear in the next room* alongside the new cards.
*   **Shields**: Equippable armor that provides a temporary health buffer (between 3-10 extra HP). 
    *   *Mechanic*: Shield HP cannot be regenerated or healed, and it does not refresh after a level ends. It absorbs damage until it breaks, and then it is gone permanently.

### Rare
*   **Transformation Consumables**: Rare items (like the Alchemist's Stone, Banishing Seal, or Cloning Scroll) that allow the player to permanently alter a card mid-run. See [[Scoundrel Deck Mechanics]] for more details on how these affect the deck.

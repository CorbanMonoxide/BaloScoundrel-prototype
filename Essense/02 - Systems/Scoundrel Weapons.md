# Scoundrel Weapons

The weapon deck in Scoundrel Balatro consists of 9 cards (Diamonds 2-Ace), each representing a unique weapon with distinct thematic and mechanical flavor. Weapons serve as multipliers in combat and degrade when used to kill monsters.

## Design Notes


## Integration Notes

Weapons are equipped one at a time. When you kill a monster, your weapon's multiplier degrades to the monster's value. The weapon names provide flavor and type-matching strategic depth; mechanically, face value (2-14) determines base damage and weapon type determines matchup modifiers.

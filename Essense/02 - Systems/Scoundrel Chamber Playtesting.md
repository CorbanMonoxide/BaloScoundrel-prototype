# Scoundrel Chamber Playtesting

Tracking deck experiments for [[Scoundrel Balatro]] chamber design.

---

## Chamber 1 — Experiments

### Deck v1.0
**Composition (16 cards):**
- Monsters (8): 2♣, 3♣, 4♣, 5♣, 6♣, 7♠, 8♠, 2♠
- Weapons (4): 3♦, 5♦, 6♦, 8♦
- Potions (4): 2♥, 4♥, 6♥, 8♥

**Results:**
- ❌ Too easy — clearing in ~1 minute, usually at full HP
- Too many rooms with 2 weapons + 2 potions
- Not enough monster pressure

### Deck v1.3
**Changes from v1.2:** Weapons changed from 6♦/7♦/8♦ to 2♦/3♦/5♦ — much weaker, forces harder combat decisions.

**Composition (16 cards):**
- Monsters (10): 2♠, 2♣, 3♠, 4♣, 5♠, 5♣, 6♠, 7♠, 8♣, 10♣
- Weapons (3): 2♦, 3♦, 5♦
- Potions (3): 2♥, 4♥, 6♥

**Results:**
- ✅ 3 wins / 1 loss across 4 runs (~75% win rate)
- Winning runs finished at ~6-7 HP — pressured but survivable
- **Verdict: LOCKED IN as Chamber 1 deck**

---

## Notes & Observations
- Potion density is a major difficulty lever — fewer potions = less forgiveness
- Weapon/monster ratio matters more than raw monster values at low value ranges

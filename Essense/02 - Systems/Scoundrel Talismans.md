# Scoundrel Talismans

This is the running list of Talismans for Scoundrel Balatro. Talismans provide persistent modifiers that alter scoring, combat, and economy.

## Common
1. **Tempered Steel**: The first time an equipped Weapon is used to kill a monster, it does not degrade
2. **Quick Feet**: Incoming dmg Reduced by 2.
3. **Pickpocket**: Defeating a monster barehanded yields +2 Gold.
4. **Sharp Blades**: Each kill with a sharp weapon adds 5 mult
5. **Heavy Hitter**: Each kill with blunt weapon adds 5 mult
6. **Piercing Blows**: Each kill with a piercing weapon adds 5 mult
7. **Grave Digger**: killing zombies gives +10 essence
8. **Ghost Buster**: Killing ghosts gives +15 essence
9. **Abomination Cleanser**: Killing demons and dragons gives+25 essence 
10. **Lich Vanquisher**: Killing Lichs gives +50 essence
11. **Chump Change**: Killing monsters with strength 6 or lower grants +1 Gold.
12. **Desperate Blows**: Weapons under 50% durability add 2 mult when killing a monster
13. **Copper Talisman**: Multiplies mult by 2x when scored at end of room
14. **Essential Talisman** :+50 Essence when scored at end of room
15. **Tally Man** : +4 mult added per kill

## Uncommon
1. **Expert Blacksmith**: Weapons adds two to durability after a monster fight resolves
2. **Bounty Hunter**: Killing a monster with a value of 10 or higher grants +5 bonus Gold.
3. **Steel Talisman**: x2 mult when scored at end of room
4. **Fists of Iron**: kills with bare hands +15 mult
5.  **Intimidation**: The last monster left in a room will decrease by 2 strength when it spawns in the next room (It will retain creature type).
6. **Coward's Charm**: Fleeing grants 100 essence for the amount of times you've fled this run
7. **Sharp Senses**: Kills with sharp weapons +10 mult
8. **Heavy Thoughts**: Kills with blunt weapons +10 mult
9. **Piercing Screams**:  Kills with piercing weapons +10 mult
10. **Strength Potions**: Held potions add their value to mult
11. **Silver Talisman**: Multiplies mult by 3x when scored at the end of the room
12. **Blood Money**: Earn 1 gold for every damage taken
13. **Evasion Tactics**: Allows the player to flee from two consecutive rooms once per chamber. Normally, you can only flee from one room before the flee button disappears for the next room. With this talisman, you can flee twice in a row (e.g., flee Room 3, then flee Room 4). *Resets at the start of each new chamber.*
14. **Smash and Grab** Earn 1 gold for each room that the player flees from cumulatively. 

## Rare
13. **Blood Vial**: Excess healing from Potions at full HP is converted into temporary Shield HP (capped at 30 HP).
14. **Undying**: Revives the player to 5 HP immediately after a lethal strike. *Destroyed upon use.*
15. **Gold Talisman**: Multiplies mult by 4x when scored at the end of the room
16. **Monk**: Kills with bare hands give x5 mult
17. **Blade Master**: Kills with sharp weapons give x4 mult
18. **Staff Master** : Kills with piercing weapons give x3 mult
19. **Hammer Master**: Kills with blunt weapons give x2 mult

# Scoundrel Deck Mechanics

In [[Scoundrel Balatro]], the player's deck is a persistent, evolving pool of cards. However, unlike traditional deckbuilders, **deck manipulation is secondary**. The game emphasizes survival, exploration, and adapting to RNG rather than mathematically perfecting a deck. 

## The Starting Deck
The game begins with the standard 44-card Scoundrel deck:
- **26 Monsters** (Clubs & Spades)
- **9 Weapons** (Diamonds)
- **9 Potions** (Hearts)

## Expanding the Deck
The deck grows naturally as the run progresses:
- **Shop & Chests:** When a player acquires a new Weapon or Potion from the [[Scoundrel Shop]] or a Chest, it is permanently shuffled into their deck for the remainder of the run.
- **Dungeon Progression (Scaling):** Between full Dungeons (levels), the game automatically injects new Monsters (including Cursed variants or higher-value threats) into the deck. This increases the difficulty and bloats the deck, forcing the player to adapt to worse odds over time.

## Transformation Consumables
Because standard "deck fixing" (like paying to remove cards at a shop) is restricted, players must rely on mid-run Consumables to manipulate the deck. These items are used directly on cards currently visible in a Room:
- **Alchemist's Stone:** Transforms a Monster card in the current room into a Potion of equal value permanently.
- **Banishing Seal:** Permanently destroys one card in the current room.
- **Cloning Scroll:** Creates a permanent duplicate of one card in the current room.

These consumables are **extremely rare** and require the player to make tactical decisions in the heat of a chamber, rather than safely optimizing between rounds. (Note: Their appearance rate can be increased by purchasing specific Magic upgrades in the shop).
